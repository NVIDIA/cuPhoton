# Sources and model assumptions · Model Sky

All astronomical arrays and instrument inputs are generated by `model_sky.py`
and `scene.py`. No observational pixels, catalogs, coordinate solutions,
measured PSFs, masks, calibration, filter curves or trained models are inputs.
The modeled arrays and example code are provided under Apache-2.0.
The four overview SVGs in `assets/` are original teaching schematics.

The sky is a fixed noiseless field of fictional stars. Each exposure receives
independent source Poisson and Gaussian sky/read noise, after seeing, dither
and transparency. Pixels use nJy; variance uses nJy². The 4 m telescope,
560–700 nm top-hat band at 40% total efficiency, 0.2 arcsec/pixel, 20.5 AB
mag/arcsec² sky and 5 electron read noise are explicit models. None describes
an actual instrument. A 3100 K Planck spectrum sets the brightness at
146.42 pc for radius 0.2162 solar. It omits M-dwarf molecular absorption and
extinction. The illustrative quadratic limb-darkening law (0.6, 0.15) is
independently declared; it is not derived from uniform blackbody emission.
The orbit is circular and the geometry fixed during radius/time fitting.

- [Charbonneau et al. (2009), GJ 1214 discovery](https://arxiv.org/abs/0912.3229)
  inspires the story; our simulated star is at a different distance.
- [Updated GJ 1214 parameters](https://arxiv.org/html/2402.05991v2) inform the
  stellar radius and illustrative orbital geometry.
- [Mandel & Agol (2002)](https://arxiv.org/abs/astro-ph/0210099) and
  [Kreidberg (2015), batman](https://arxiv.org/abs/1507.08285) describe physical
  limb-darkened transit models. We integrate over each 30 second exposure.
- [Alard & Lupton (1998)](https://arxiv.org/abs/astro-ph/9712287) provides the
  image-subtraction context. Actual cuPhoton xRep, xPois and xFit GPU calls
  reproject planes, fit kernels and measure stars in this example.
- [Tyson et al. (2020)](https://doi.org/10.3847/1538-3881/abba3e) discusses
  real satellite trails; ours is a separately modeled teaching approximation.
- [Bryson et al. (2013)](https://arxiv.org/abs/1303.0052) explains centroid
  vetting; a displaced source can explain a dip. A transit-like candidate
  still needs repeated events, spectra and other follow-up for confirmation.
- [AAVSO exoplanet photometry](https://aavso.org/exoplanet-photometry/) connects
  calibrated light curves to follow-up work.
- [cuPhoton documentation](https://github.com/NVIDIA/cuPhoton/tree/main/docs)
  describes the product interfaces. xScan is explicitly omitted here; no
  classifier weights, scores or training stamps are included.
- [Bokeh standalone documents](https://docs.bokeh.org/en/latest/docs/user_guide/output/embed.html)
  explain embedded offline interactions. Inspecting a view does not rerun fits.

Truth is stored separately and revealed only after measurement. Five noise
realizations test conditional uncertainties, not all astrophysical systematics.
Incorrect PSFs and limb-darkening changes probe model sensitivity; they do not
fully marginalize over real atmosphere or instrument uncertainty.
